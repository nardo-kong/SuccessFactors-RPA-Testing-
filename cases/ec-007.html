Promotion Management — Off-Cycle

Validates the end-to-end off-cycle promotion workflow: position and compensation changes are submitted, routed through a multi-level approval chain, and confirmed in the employee's employment history upon completion.

Test Objective

Validate that an off-cycle promotion request can be submitted through the SAP SuccessFactors interface, routed through the complete multi-level approval workflow, and that all position and compensation changes are accurately reflected in the employee's job history upon final approval.

Position Change Compensation Update Multi-Approver Workflow Employment History Verification
📋
Prerequisites
✓
Active employee record exists in the system with valid employment and compensation information
✓
HR operator account has proxy capability enabled and permission to initiate job and compensation changes
✓
Target position for the promotion is pre-created, active, and available in the Position Org Chart
✓
Promotion approval workflow is configured with all required approvers and routing rules in place
✓
Test data (employee identifier, target position, effective date, compensation amounts) loaded in Data Service
🔬
Test Steps
1
GIVEN
System Authentication
Log in to SAP SuccessFactors via the configured tenant URL. RPA service account credentials are retrieved securely from the UiPath Orchestrator credential store at runtime.
2
GIVEN
Proxy as HR Operator
Use the proxy function to adopt the identity of the designated HR Business Facing operator who will initiate the promotion request. Verify the proxy is active and correct user context is displayed in the system header.
3
WHEN
Navigate to Employee Profile
Use the People search function to locate the target employee by their Employee ID. Open the full People Profile page and navigate to the General Information section.
4
WHEN
Initiate Job Change Action
From the employee profile, select "Take Action" and choose "Change Job and Compensation Info". Set the effective date for the promotion as specified in the test data.
5
WHEN
Update Target Position
In the Position Information section, select the new target position from the position picker. Confirm that the position details (title, code) are correctly populated. Save the position change.
✓ Change Position Saved
6
WHEN
Update Cash Allowance
In the Compensation section, update the Cash Allowance component to the new promotion amount as specified in the test data. Save the compensation change.
✓ Change Allowance Saved
7
WHEN
Submit Promotion Request
Review all populated fields including contract start date, Rostered/Non-Rostered status, and RTA flag. Click "Save" to generate the submission confirmation dialog. Confirm submission. The system retrieves the full approval chain. Capture and verify the approver list.
✓ GetApprovalList
8
WHEN
Approval — Step 1 (HR Business Facing)
Proxy as the first approver in the chain (HR Business Facing role). Navigate to pending workflow requests. Locate the promotion approval for the target employee. Review all position and compensation details. Click Approve.
✓ Approver 1 Approval Successful
9
WHEN
Approval — Steps 2 through 6 (Sequential)
Sequentially proxy as each of the remaining approvers in the workflow chain (Approvers 2–6, including IT Director, People & Organisational Development lead, Quality Management head, HRSS Mobility, and CEO office). At each step: locate the pending request, review details, and approve. The system handles deduplication of approvers where the same person appears in multiple steps.
✓ Approver 2 Successful ✓ Approver 3 Successful ✓ Approver 4 Successful ✓ Approver 5 Successful ✓ Approver 6 Successful
10
THEN
Return to HR Operator Context & Navigate to Profile
Proxy back to the original HR operator context. Navigate to the target employee's People Profile → Employment Information → Job Information section.
11
THEN
Verify Promotion Completed in System
Open the Job Information history panel. Verify: (1) A new record dated with the promotion effective date is visible. (2) Position title shows the new promoted position. (3) Job classification fields reflect the new grade. (4) Pay Grade and Pay Grade Label are updated. (5) The workflow status is "Completed". Screenshot captured as evidence.
✓ Promotion Finished
✅
Expected Results
✅
Position and Compensation Updated
The position change and cash allowance update are saved without any validation errors. All fields reflect the new promotion data.
🔄
Complete Approval Chain Processed
The promotion request successfully routes through all configured approvers (6 total) without interruption. Duplicate approver steps are handled and skipped correctly.
📋
Employment History Updated
The employee's Job Information History shows a new promotion record with the correct effective date, updated position title, grade, and pay grade label.
📊
All Assertions Pass
All 11 assertion checks pass (11/11). Workflow status transitions to "Completed" after the final approval step.
