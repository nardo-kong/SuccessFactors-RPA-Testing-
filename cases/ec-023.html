Contract Management — Hong Kong (HKG) Renewal

Validates the contract renewal workflow for Hong Kong entity employees: the HR operator selects the contract renewal action, enters the new contract end date, and the request is processed through a multi-level approval chain before the contract record is updated.

Test Objective

Validate that the contract renewal process for Hong Kong entity employees can be executed end-to-end through automation. The test verifies that the correct contract action is triggered, the new contract end date is entered and saved, the approval workflow is correctly generated and routed, and the employee's contract record reflects the renewed end date upon completion of all approvals.

Contract Renewal HKG Legal Entity Multi-Level Approval
📋
Prerequisites
✓
Target employee belongs to the Hong Kong (HKG) legal entity and has a fixed-term contract with an upcoming expiry date
✓
Contract renewal workflow is configured and the "ContractRenew" action type is available for the employee's employment type
✓
HR operator has proxy capability and permission to initiate contract renewal actions for HKG employees
✓
Approval chain for contract renewals is configured with all required approvers (minimum 5 levels)
✓
New contract end date and employee identifier loaded in UiPath Data Service as test parameters
🔬
Test Steps
1
GIVEN
System Authentication
Navigate to the SAP SuccessFactors tenant URL. Retrieve service account credentials from Orchestrator. Log in and verify the home dashboard loads successfully.
2
GIVEN
Proxy as HR Operator (HKG)
Use the proxy function to adopt the designated HKG HR operator identity. Verify the user context reflects the correct legal entity scope before proceeding.
3
WHEN
Navigate to Employee Profile
Search for the target employee by Employee ID. Open their People Profile and navigate to the Employment Information section.
4
WHEN
Select Contract Renewal Action
Click "Take Action" on the employee profile. From the action list, select "Contract Renewal" (ContractRenew). Verify the contract renewal form is opened with the current contract details pre-populated.
✓ Select ContractRenew
5
WHEN
Enter New Contract End Date
In the Contract Information section, update the Contract End Date field with the new renewal end date as specified in the test data. Verify the date is accepted and the form displays the updated value without validation errors.
✓ Contract End Date Entered
6
WHEN
Submit — Retrieve Approval List and Approver
Review the contract renewal summary and click Submit. The system builds the approval routing chain. Capture and verify the complete approver list, and retrieve the first approver's identity for the approval loop.
✓ GetApprovalList✓ GetApprover
7
WHEN
Approval Steps 1 through 5
Sequentially proxy as each approver in the workflow chain (5 approval steps). At each step: proxy as the approver → navigate to workflow inbox → locate the contract renewal request → review details → click Approve. Continue until all approval steps in the chain are completed.
✓ Approve Step 1 Successful ✓ Approve Step 2 Successful ✓ Approve Step 3 Successful ✓ Approve Step 4 Successful ✓ Approve Step 5 Successful
8
THEN
Verify Contract Renewal Completed
Return to the employee's Employment Information. Open the contract details and verify: (1) The new contract end date is displayed. (2) The workflow status shows "Completed". (3) The change history records the renewal event. Screenshot captured as evidence.
✓ Contract Renewal Finished
✅
Expected Results
Contract action triggered correctly: The ContractRenew action type is available and selected successfully. The renewal form opens with current contract data pre-filled.
New end date accepted: The new contract end date is validated and saved. No date format or business rule errors are raised.
Full approval chain completed: All 5 approvers successfully approve the request in sequence. The workflow completes without interruption.
Contract record updated: The employee's contract end date reflects the new renewal date. All 11 assertions pass (11/11).
